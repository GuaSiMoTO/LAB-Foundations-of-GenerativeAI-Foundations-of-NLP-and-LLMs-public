One day, a poor boy named Sam found a strange paintbrush in the forest. When he drew an apple on a rock, it turned real! Excited, he painted bread for his hungry family, coins for the struggling villagers, and toys for the children.

But the greedy mayor heard of the magic brush and demanded it. Sam refused, so the mayor locked him up. With his last stroke, Sam painted a key to escape.

The furious mayor chased him, so Sam quickly painted a river, then a boat to cross it. The mayor, trying to follow, fell in and gave up.

Sam returned home, using the brush only to help others. The village prospered, and the mayor, humbled, became kinder.
